## Challenge

A B2B SaaS's enterprise customers kept emailing support for usage reports. The data existed but lived in an internal admin panel customers couldn't see.

## Solution

We designed and shipped an embedded, customer-facing analytics dashboard inside their existing Next.js product ~ usage trends, exports, per-account drilldowns ~ instrumented so their team could watch adoption. Built alongside their two engineers, in their codebase.

## Results

Shipped in six weeks. Reporting-related support tickets dropped 31% in the first quarter, and every enterprise account activated the dashboard within 30 days.
