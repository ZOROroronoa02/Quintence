## Challenge

A 60-person fintech closed the books in spreadsheets: Stripe exports, bank statements, and ledger dumps stitched by hand. The monthly close took nine working days, and product, finance, and the board each had a different revenue number.

## Solution

We modeled a single warehouse on dbt and built idempotent Dagster pipelines for 14 sources with data-quality checks and freshness SLAs. A Metabase layer gave every metric one definition. Finance now reviews exceptions instead of assembling data.

## Results

The close dropped from nine days to two. Roughly 40 hours of monthly manual reporting disappeared, and six months in, the warehouse has had zero silent failures ~ problems alert before anyone asks.
